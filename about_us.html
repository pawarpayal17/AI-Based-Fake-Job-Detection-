About Our AI Fake Job Detection System

Protecting job seekers from online fraud using Artificial Intelligence.

Our Mission

Our mission is to prevent job seekers from falling victim to fake job postings. Using Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing, our system analyzes job descriptions and predicts whether a job is Real or Fake.

Technology Used

  • Python
  • Django Framework
  • Scikit-learn
  • TF-IDF Vectorization
  • Logistic Regression
  • SQLite Database

Why This Project?

Online job fraud is increasing rapidly. This platform helps users verify job postings before applying, reducing financial and personal risk.
